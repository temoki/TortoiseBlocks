THE TORTOISE, REDRAWN — AND WALKING

The tortoise on your table now looks like the one on the app icon, and it walks as it draws.

• The 3D tortoise has been redrawn after the app icon: a big round head, bright eyes and a smile, a beret, and a tail that is a paintbrush dipped in paint.
• While it draws, the tortoise walks: its legs step, and it rocks from side to side as it goes.
