NOW ON IPHONE

Tortoise Blocks runs on iPhone, and on iPad the way back to your drawings is where you look for it.

• On iPhone, the program fills the screen, and the blocks and the drawing open from the buttons along the bottom.
• On iPhone, tapping a drawing in the Files app opens it straight away.
• On iPad, a Drawings button now sits at the top left while a drawing is open, so there is always a way back to the list.
• Other small fixes and internal improvements.
