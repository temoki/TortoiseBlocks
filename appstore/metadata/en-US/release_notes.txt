BLOCKS YOU MAKE YOURSELF

Give a block a name, put whatever you like inside it, and call it from anywhere in your program. A block can call itself, too — which is all it takes to draw a tree.

• A new sample draws a fractal tree, so you can see a block calling itself before you build one.
• Every block now carries one ⋯ menu: delete it, move it up or down, or give an "if" its "otherwise".
• Tap the trash can to clear the whole program at once. You can undo it.
• Block labels stay on one line in narrower windows.

ON APPLE VISION PRO

Tortoise Blocks now runs on Apple Vision Pro, as a viewer for the drawings you make here. Open one and it goes on the table in front of you, at whatever size you like, with the tortoise walking across the paper as the picture appears. Your drawings are the same documents on every device.
