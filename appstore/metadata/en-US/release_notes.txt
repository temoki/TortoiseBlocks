BLOCKS THAT MOVE LIKE BLOCKS

Building a program now feels more like handling real blocks.

• Drag a block and it lifts off looking just like itself — and a block with others inside carries them along.
• The rows part to show exactly where your block will land, and a block that holds others lights up in its own colour when you drop into it.
• The block that is running lifts gently instead of wearing a dark outline.
• Blocks slide into place when you add, move, delete or undo, and the program scrolls to the block you just added.
• The trash can bounces when you throw a block in, and the whole circle can be tapped.
• All of it follows Reduce Motion in Settings.
